# What this site is - and what it must never become

The whole point of this blog is calm. It is easy to lose that one reasonable-sounding
addition at a time. This file is the guardrail. Read it before adding anything.

## The spirit

**The job is not to fight the system - it is to quietly help the people.** No arguing,
no outrage, no taking sides. Just clear, calm help for anyone who wants to run AI on
their own machine. Every choice on this site should serve that, and nothing else.

## What it is

- **Quiet.** A deep, calm dark theme; monospace headings, easy-to-read body. Nothing blinks, pops, or moves.
- **Static.** Plain HTML files served by Caddy. No database, no login, no program running.
- **Owned.** The content is yours, in git. Readers subscribe by RSS - no algorithm decides what they see.
- **Plain.** Short posts, plain words. Two sticker labels tell you how each post was written.

## What is bad for this site (resist these)

- **No comments.** They bring spam, moderation, and a database - the exact noise we avoid. If someone wants to reply, they can email.
- **No newsletter service (yet).** RSS already lets people follow with zero infrastructure. Add a newsletter only if people actually ask for one.
- **No Google Analytics or ad/tracking scripts.** Watching readers contradicts everything the site stands for. Default to measuring nothing. If ever curious, read server logs or self-host a privacy-friendly counter.
- **No JavaScript framework.** The site is static HTML on purpose. The only script is the tiny search on `/search/`. Keep it that way.
- **Only two authorship labels: Human and Human + AI.** Do not add a third. The charm is that there are only two.
- **No video hosted or embedded on the site.** Videos live on your YouTube channel - link to them, and never paste an embed iframe (an embed loads Google's trackers). Keep the pages themselves light and text-first.
- **Keep the top nav to three sections** (Beginner, Advanced, Wellbeing) plus the search icon. About lives in the footer.
- **No cookie banners, popups, modals, or "subscribe" overlays.** Ever.

## The test before adding anything

Ask: *does this make the site quieter, or louder?* If louder, the answer is no - even
if the feature is good in general. It is not good **for this site**.
