date = 2026-07-15

Two small changes, no apps to install, nothing blocked.

**One - turn your screen to greyscale.** A grey feed is far less magnetic than a bright one. The colour is part of the pull; take it away and the phone quietly loses its grip.

**Two - take the noisy apps off your home screen.** Not delete - just move them a search away. The extra half-second is enough to turn a reflex back into a choice.

Try it for one evening. If it does nothing for you, switch it back in a minute. But most people notice the room gets a little quieter.
