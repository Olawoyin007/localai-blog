A "local" AI is one that runs on your own computer, not on someone else's servers far away.

That one change carries most of the weight of this whole site. When the model lives on your machine, no one can switch it off for you, nothing you type leaves the room, and there is no meter running. It keeps working on a train, on a plane, in a blackout.

You do not need special hardware to try it. A recent laptop can run a small model today - slower than the big cloud ones, but real, and yours. Start small, get a feel for it, and grow from there.

That is the whole idea: a capable model you own outright, sitting quietly on your own machine.
