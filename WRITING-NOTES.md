# Writing notes - what goes in each section

A quick reminder to myself when I sit down to write. Pick the section by asking:
*who is this for, and what do they walk away able to do?*

---

## Beginner

**Who:** someone who has never run AI on their own machine. Maybe a little nervous.

**Goal:** they finish the post able to do one small thing, or understanding one idea,
with no jargon left unexplained.

**Tone:** patient, plain words, one step at a time. Assume nothing.

**Good post ideas:**
- "What a local model actually is" (no maths).
- "Do you even need a GPU?"
- "Your first model in 10 minutes."
- "Which model should I start with?"
- Anything where the win is: *they stop being scared of it.*

**Rule of thumb:** if a beginner would have to Google a word, either explain it in
the sentence or cut it.

---

## Advanced

**Who:** someone who already runs models and wants to go further.

**Goal:** they learn a real technique, trade-off, or setup they didn't have before.

**Tone:** still calm and plain, but you can assume the basics. You can name tools
(vLLM, ollama, quantization) without a paragraph of setup.

**Good post ideas:**
- "Choosing a quantization" (what the numbers cost you).
- "Serving models to the whole house."
- "Vision models locally."
- "When to use vLLM vs ollama."
- Anything with a real trade-off to weigh.

**Rule of thumb:** a beginner might not follow it, and that's fine. But it must still
be *plainly written* - advanced is not an excuse for a wall of jargon.

---

## Wellbeing

**Who:** anyone who feels their screens are a bit much. Not necessarily technical.

**Goal:** one small, honest change they can make for a calmer relationship with tech.

**Tone:** gentle, honest, never preachy. No fear, no outrage. Just quiet help.

**Good post ideas:**
- "Two settings for calmer evenings."
- "The feed is designed - here's how."
- "One notification change that helped me."
- Small, doable things. Not lectures.

**Rule of thumb:** if it makes the reader feel judged or scared, it's wrong for this
section. The job is to quietly help, not to fight the system.

---

## Before you hit publish (any section)

- One idea per post. If it's two ideas, it's two posts.
- Short sentences. Read it out loud - if you run out of breath, cut it.
- Set the sticker: `authorship = "organic"` (all you) or `"human-ai"` (with AI help).
- Delete the `draft = true` line when it's ready.
- Ask the PRINCIPLES.md test: *does this make the site quieter, or louder?*
